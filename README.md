## 🖼️ How to Put Your Own Photos, Certificates & Teammates (2 Easy Ways)

### Method 1: Instant Click & Upload (Direct in Browser)
1. Open [`index.html`](file:///c:/Users/Dell/Downloads/bcs-custom-3d-portfolio/index.html) in your browser.
2. On any photo (Front Page, About Me, or Teammate cards), click the **"Change Photo"** button at the top-right corner.
3. Select any photo from your computer, and it will immediately render live in 3D!

### Method 2: Save Images in the `images/` Folder
Create an `images` subfolder in `bcs-custom-3d-portfolio/` and save your files with these names:
- **`images/hero-photo.jpg`** — Your main front-page photo.
- **`images/about-photo.jpg`** — Your about me profile photo.
- **`images/cert-hackathon.jpg`** — Your hackathon certificate image.
- **`images/cert-dsa.jpg`** — Your DSA / NPTEL / Course certificate image.
- **`images/cert-web.jpg`** — Your Web development / LeetCode badge.
- **`images/team-1.jpg`** — Teammate 1 Photo (Backend / Java).
- **`images/team-2.jpg`** — Teammate 2 Photo (Frontend / React).
- **`images/team-3.jpg`** — Teammate 3 Photo (Cloud / DevOps).
